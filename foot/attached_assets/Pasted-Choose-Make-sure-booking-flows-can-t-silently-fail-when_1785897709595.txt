Choose “Make sure booking flows can't silently fail when the database is under pressure.” That is the best next task because it protects the core system reliability before adding payments or credential workflows, both of which are higher-level features that depend on a stable booking pipeline.

Why this first
If the database is under pressure and booking writes fail silently, users can lose trust fast and you may create duplicate or half-complete bookings. Hardening the booking flow now is the right move because it ensures the system degrades safely instead of pretending a change succeeded.

Why not payments yet
“Let clients pay for confirmed bookings without leaving the app” is important, but payment flow should come after the booking path is stress-tested and confirmed stable. Otherwise you risk mixing checkout errors with booking integrity issues and making debugging much harder.

Why not credentials yet
“Let providers submit credentials for admin approval” is also valuable, but it’s a trust/ops workflow that should come after the system is reliable under load. Booking reliability is the foundation; verification can layer on after that.
