# Provider FAQ — Foot pilot

**How much does this cost me?**
Nothing during the pilot. You keep 100% of what clients pay you.

**How do clients pay?**
Directly to you — cash or e-transfer at the visit, same as today. The app
doesn't touch money yet.

**How do clients find my page?**
Only through the link/QR you share, or the marketplace list. Share the link in
your Instagram bio, texts, Facebook, or your website — we record which channel
each booking came from so you can see what works.

**A client outside my area tried to book — what happens?**
They're told you don't serve that area before they ever pick a service. Add or
remove postal areas anytime under Service area; changes apply to new bookings,
never to already-confirmed visits.

**Can I change a confirmed time?**
You can **propose** a new time with a reason. The client gets asked and must
confirm — their confirmed time never changes without their consent. If they
decline, the original time stands (or you can cancel with a reason).

**What if the client isn't home?**
After the appointment time passes, open the booking and tap **No-show**. It's
recorded with the time and never silently deleted. No fee is charged to anyone
during the pilot.

**What's the cancellation policy clients see?**
Free cancellation until **24 hours** before the visit; later cancellations are
recorded as late (no fee in the pilot). If **you** cancel, it never counts
against the client — you'll be asked for a structured reason (illness,
emergency, schedule conflict, …). Your free-text notes stay private to support.

**Why is there a gap around my bookings?**
A 30-minute travel/setup buffer is protected before and after every visit so
you're never double-booked back-to-back across town.

**Can I unpublish my page?**
Yes, anytime from the dashboard. Your link stays reserved and comes back when
you republish.

**Times look right for clients in other timezones?**
Yes — all times are shown in Toronto time everywhere, with a label.

**Something's broken / a dispute with a client — who do I contact?**
The **Need help?** link at the bottom of every portal page. We respond within
24 hours. Disputes about a finished booking can also be escalated by either
side straight from the booking, and we mediate.

**What happens after the pilot?**
Week 5 is a short feedback chat. If you want to keep using Foot, you keep your
page, link, history, and reviews — nothing is deleted.
