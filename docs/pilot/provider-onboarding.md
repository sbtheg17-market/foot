# Welcome to the Foot pilot — provider onboarding

Welcome! You're one of **5 foot-care providers** in our Southern Ontario pilot
(St. Catharines → Oakville). For 2–5 weeks you'll take real bookings through
your own booking page. **The pilot is free for you — you keep 100% of what
clients pay.** Clients pay you directly (cash or e-transfer), exactly like today.

## What you get

- A personal booking page at `…/book/your-name` you can share anywhere
  (Instagram bio, texts, Facebook, your website, a printable QR card).
- Automatic service-area checks — clients outside the areas you serve are told
  before they can book.
- A 30-minute travel/setup buffer protected around every appointment.
- Clear, fair cancellation and no-show handling (24-hour notice policy shown
  to clients up front).
- Direct support from the pilot team (24-hour response).

## Week-by-week

| Week | What happens |
|---|---|
| 1 | Onboarding: account, service area, services, availability, publish, share |
| 2–4 | Active bookings — you deliver visits and use the app day-to-day |
| 5 | Wrap-up: 30-minute feedback chat + "would you keep using this?" |

## Setup (about 15 minutes)

Follow `provider-setup-checklist.md` — the short version:

1. **Sign in** with the account we created for you.
2. **Service area** — add the postal areas (first 3 characters, e.g. `L2R`,
   `L6H`) you actually serve. Clients outside them can't book you.
3. **Services** — confirm your pilot menu (routine nail care, callus
   debridement, diabetic foot check, thick nail reduction, fungal nail
   assessment) with your prices ($50–90) and durations (30–45 min).
4. **Availability** — tap the "9–5 weekdays" preset, then adjust to your
   real week.
5. **Publish your booking page** from the dashboard.
6. **Share your link** — copy it into your Instagram bio, text it to 5 regular
   clients, and print the QR card for your kit bag.

## During the pilot

- **New request** → you get a notification → Accept it (or decline with a reason).
- **Need to move a visit?** → propose a new time — the client confirms it
  (their time never changes without their OK).
- **Client didn't answer the door?** → after the visit time passes, mark
  **No-show** on the booking. It's recorded fairly.
- **Need to cancel?** → cancel with a reason. It never counts against the client.
- **Anything weird or a dispute?** → the **"Need help?"** link at the bottom of
  every portal page reaches us; we reply within 24 hours.

## What we measure (so you know)

Activation, shares, bookings, completions, cancellations, no-shows, where your
bookings come from (Instagram / QR / text / Facebook / website), and — most
importantly — whether you'd keep using it after week 5.

## Support
