# Provider setup checklist — Foot pilot

Print this or keep it open. Everything is in the provider portal after sign-in.
Target: done in ~15 minutes.

## Before you start
- [ ] You received your sign-in email + temporary password
- [ ] You can sign in at `/login` on your phone

## 1. Service area (Provider → Service area)
- [ ] Country/province: Canada / Ontario
- [ ] Add every postal area you serve — the **first 3 characters** of postal
      codes, e.g. `L2R`, `L2T` (St. Catharines), `L6H`, `L6J` (Oakville)
- [ ] Add a short public description (e.g. "Serving St. Catharines to Oakville")
- [ ] Check: enter one of YOUR area codes on your own booking page → "serves your area"
- [ ] Check: enter `K1A 0A6` (Ottawa) → politely declined

## 2. Services (Provider → Services)
- [ ] Routine Nail Care — 30–45 min — $60–80
- [ ] Callus Debridement — 30 min — $50–70
- [ ] Diabetic Foot Check — 30–45 min — $70–90
- [ ] Thick Nail Reduction — 45 min — $70–90
- [ ] Fungal Nail Assessment — 30 min — $50–60
- [ ] Anything you don't offer is toggled off
      (pilot scope: no wound care, orthotics, or multi-visit packages)

## 3. Availability (Provider → Availability)
- [ ] Tap the **9–5 weekdays** preset
- [ ] Adjust to your real week (the system keeps a 30-min travel buffer
      between visits automatically)

## 4. Profile
- [ ] Photo, title, short bio, city
- [ ] Credentials submitted/verified (badge shows on your page)

## 5. Publish and share (Provider → Dashboard)
- [ ] **Publish booking page** — note your link `…/book/your-name`
- [ ] Copy the link into your Instagram bio
- [ ] Text the link to at least 5 regular clients
- [ ] Open the **QR card** and print it for your kit bag
- [ ] Test: open your link in your phone's browser and walk through a fake
      booking up to the last step (don't submit, or submit + cancel — both fine)

## 6. Know the day-to-day
- [ ] You know where new requests appear (Bookings tab) and how to Accept
- [ ] You know how to propose a new time (client must confirm)
- [ ] You know the No-show button appears only after a visit time has passed
- [ ] You found the **Need help?** link in the portal footer

**Done?** Tell us "I'm live" via the support link — that counts as activation. 🎉
